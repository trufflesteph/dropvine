Questions

Good to know.

Do I need a website?

No. Your drop page is your shop. Share the link on Instagram, Facebook, or by text.

How do customers pay?

Directly to you on Venmo. Dropvine shows them the amount and a short note to include, so you can match each payment to its order. We recommend a Venmo business profile.

What if someone orders and never pays?

Their order shows as unpaid and holds those items. If you think they've walked away, cancel it. The items go back on sale and the customer gets an email.

Do I need a customer list to start?

No. Share your drop link anywhere. If you have a list, upload it and Dropvine emails it for you.

What happens after the free trial?

Near the end of your 30 days, we'll reach out so you can pick the plan that fits. Nothing is charged automatically.

Does Dropvine handle cottage food rules?

No. You're responsible for following your state's rules on labeling and selling. Dropvine is the tool you sell with.

I'm not a baker. Can I use Dropvine?

Yes. It works for any small-batch maker who sells in drops.
